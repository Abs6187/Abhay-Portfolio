About Me

Hi, I'm Abhay Gupta — a dedicated computer science undergraduate pursuing dual degrees at IIT Madras (BS in Data Science & Applications) and Shri Ram Institute of Technology, Jabalpur (BTech in Computer Science).

I specialize in building intelligent full-stack web applications that combine modern frontend frameworks with cutting-edge AI capabilities. My work spans computer vision, natural language processing, multi-agent AI systems, and production-grade web applications.

I'm currently working as an SDE at APMOSYS and previously completed training as an Apprentice (Trainee Engineer) at Xebia, working on Appian low-code automation and AngularJS-based UI components.
